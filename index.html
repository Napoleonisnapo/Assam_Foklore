Folk Instruments->

The assamese culture consists of several intruments of several kinds which can be played.

These are some of the intruments of the assamese culture. We will talk about the Doba and its significance.

THis is the Doba one the instruments of the assamese culture. Just like the other instruments it also has it's own significance is assamese history and culture. The Doba also served communicational purposes in earlier times. The Doba would be strunck to make a rhythm to alert the villagers.

It is also a very important part of assamese culture.

it's mouth is wide with a diameter of 1.2 meter that is covered with tightly strentched animal hide taken from buffalo. This hide is the perrcussion part of this instrument. The hide is struck with stick or hand. It produces a low pitch sound. The shell part of the Doba has air in it. When the hide is struck the air inside the hollow shell vibrates, this change is percieved as sound by our ears. This hollow shell also gives the doba it's low pitched majestic sound.

It's purposes-

In temples the Doba is played two times- sunrise and sunset and also mark communal prayers. It's majestic sound is believed to scare away evil spirits.

The folk songs!

If the Assamese folklore has so many instruments surely they must have lots of songs? well, you are right aside from the bihu songs the assamese folklore has many more songs with their own purpose, beat, and rhythm. Such great works of art are worth reading about once so lets dive in.

Most of the assamese folklore songs can be catergorised into a few sects.

  1. Bihu Geet
  2. Borgeet
  3. Zikir(islamic devotional songs)
  4. Ai Naam(Prayer)
  5. Biya naam(Marriage)
  6. Nisukani Geet(Lullabies)

The bihu geet are the songs made for and used in the bihu dance and culture.

The Borgeet are the songs compiled for lord krishna in vaishnavite traditions. These express The love for Lord Krishna and depict his life.

The Zikir is an islamic practice to remember God "Allah" through repeated recitations and singing is devotional songs.

The Ai Naam are prayer compiled into songs for the Goddess of Pox(Sitala). She is a feared deity. yes, she is believed to bring SmallPox and ChickenPox and when their outbreak happen the locals would sing soft, devotional songs to appease her and get the healing.

The Nisukani Geet are lullabies for the children. These have soft notes to bring joy to the child and make them feel comforted.
